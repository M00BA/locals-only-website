About Locals Only

Locals Only is a small, warm community space in Hamburg built for people who want to connect without social anxiety or pressure. Whether you are navigating life changes, feeling isolated, or looking for genuine companionship, you belong here.

Our Commitment: This is a strict safe space. We welcome LGBTQ+ individuals, people with disabilities, refugees, introverts, and anyone who has ever felt like an outsider. Everyone is treated with dignity, kindness, and respect.

My Story

Hi there. I’m the creator of Locals Only. I am a 24-year-old Kurdish semi-straight man who came to Germany as a refugee from Tehran, Iran.

Starting over in a new country came with profound hardships and a constant, heavy struggle to meet people and find my place. Navigating life's hardest moments in isolation can feel overwhelming. That is why I built this community.

Locals Only isn’t about big crowds or superficial networking—it’s about gathering a supportive group of people around us so we can share life’s journey together, lift each other up through the hard times, and remind ourselves that nobody has to walk alone.
